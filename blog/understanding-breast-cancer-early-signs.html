Understanding Breast Cancer: Early Signs & Screening

Early detection is the single most powerful factor in successfully treating breast cancer. Knowing what changes to look out for and seeking prompt clinical evaluation can dramatically improve clinical outcomes.

Key Warning Signs

  • A painless lump or localized thickening in the breast or armpit (axilla)
  • Noticeable changes in the size, shape, or symmetry of the breast
  • Dimpling, puckering, or redness of the breast skin
  • Nipple retraction, inversion, scaling, or spontaneous discharge

Screening Methods

Modern breast screening protocols combine monthly breast self-examination (BSE), annual clinical breast examination (CBE), mammography, and diagnostic breast ultrasound.
