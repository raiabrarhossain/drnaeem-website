Thyroid Nodules: When Should You Worry?

Thyroid nodules are solid or fluid-filled lumps that form within your thyroid, a butterfly-shaped endocrine gland located at the base of your neck. Most thyroid nodules are benign (non-cancerous), but a thorough surgical evaluation is essential to rule out malignancy and maintain optimal thyroid health.

Common Symptoms to Watch

  • A noticeable lump or swelling in the front lower neck area
  • A feeling of tightness, discomfort, or difficulty swallowing
  • Unexplained voice changes, hoarseness, or throat irritation
  • Rapid growth or firming of an existing neck swelling

Diagnostic Steps

When you visit Dr. Muhammad Naeem in Faisalabad for a thyroid evaluation, the process involves:

  1. Clinical Examination: Physical palpation of the thyroid gland and cervical lymph nodes.
  2. High-Resolution Ultrasound: To assess nodule size, echogenicity, borders, and microcalcifications.
  3. Fine Needle Aspiration (FNA): A simple diagnostic procedure to sample cells if malignant features are present.
  4. Thyroid Function Tests (TFTs): Checking TSH, Free T3, and Free T4 levels.

Surgical Treatment Options

If surgery is required due to compression symptoms, rapid growth, or suspicion of malignancy, Dr. Naeem performs precise thyroidectomies (lobectomy or total thyroidectomy) preserving the recurrent laryngeal nerves and parathyroid glands.
