Preparing for Surgery: A Patient’s Guide

Preparing for a surgical procedure can feel daunting. However, knowing what steps to take beforehand helps reduce anxiety, prevents unexpected complications, and ensures a faster, smoother recovery.

1. Pre-Operative Health Clearance

  • Complete all prescribed pre-op blood investigations (CBC, Blood Grouping, LFTs, RFTs, Coagulation Profile).
  • Undergo routine chest X-rays and ECG clearance as instructed by the surgical and anesthesia team.
  • Inform Dr. Naeem about all prescription medications, herbal supplements, and blood thinners (e.g., Aspirin).

2. Fasting & Medication Guidelines

Prior to elective surgery under general or spinal anesthesia, strict fasting (NPO - Nil Per Os) is required, usually 6 to 8 hours before the procedure. Do not consume water, tea, or food during this period unless specifically approved.

3. What to Bring to the Hospital

  • All previous medical records, diagnostic imaging, and ultrasound scans.
  • CNIC / Identity documents and hospital admission files.
  • Comfortable, loose clothing and flat supportive footwear.
  • An accompanying family member to assist during hospital stay and discharge.
